So who E be? Make we find out for Isa 42:5 (READ). The person wey write this bible book make am clear say that creator na JEHOVAH!

You dey see evidence say Creator dey?

  2. The earth get atmosphere wey dey protect life. E dey protect us from harmful rays of the sun and e dey help us breathe.
  3. The earth get water wey dey support life. Water dey very important for all living things.
  4. The earth get moon wey dey stabilize our climate and tides.
  5. E get better seasons wey de benefit us.
  6. E get soil/sand wey de produce better sweet food for us.

If we compare our planet Earth to other planets wey dey this galaxy and other galaxies, we go see say our planet na one special planet. We fit even call am the

"Garden of Eden of the Solar system"
